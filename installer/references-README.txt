WallyCull references
====================

Put images here that you consider great wall art, for example your best sellers
or favorite prints. JPG, PNG, TIFF and RAW files work. 20 to 100 images are plenty.

During the next analysis WallyCull compares every photo with these references:
photos in a similar style get a higher value score.

The images stay on this PC. You can add or remove images at any time.
